🛡️ Our Commitment to Quality & Safety

At Life Plus Support Services, we hold ourselves to the highest standard — because our participants deserve nothing less.

  • Rigorous screening & hiring: All support workers undergo thorough background checks, NDIS Worker Screening Clearances, and reference verifications before joining our team.
  • Fully qualified & experienced: Our workers hold relevant qualifications in disability support, aged care, or community services, and participate in ongoing professional development.
  • Up-to-code practices: We strictly adhere to the NDIS Practice Standards and Quality & Safeguarding Framework, ensuring every service we deliver meets regulatory requirements.
  • Mandatory training: Every team member completes First Aid, Manual Handling, Infection Control, and Safeguarding training as a condition of employment.
  • Compassionate culture: We don't just hire for skills — we hire for values. Our workers are selected for their genuine care, empathy, and commitment to empowering participants.
  • Continuous improvement: Regular performance reviews, participant feedback, and internal audits ensure we maintain the highest standards of support at all times.
